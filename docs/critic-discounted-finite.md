## Source reconstruction

The source assumes finite state and action carriers, a time-independent transition distribution P and reward r∈[0,1], a stationary randomized Markov policy π and γ∈[0,1). Starting at s₀=s, actions are sampled using π and next states using P. Its value is the expectation on the infinite sampled trajectory of ∑ₜ₌₀∞ γᵗ r(sₜ,aₜ). Thus the N-reward truncation is ∑ₜ₌₀ᴺ⁻¹ γᵗ r(sₜ,aₜ): N=0 is zero, N=1 uses the undiscounted immediate reward, and γ=0 retains precisely that reward for N≥1. The state Bellman operator is Eπ[r(s,a)]+γ E_{Pπ}[V(s′)], and the source asserts its infinite-trajectory value is a fixed point.

## Findings

- The literal finite path return (lines 23–27) has zero for an empty path and immediate reward plus γ times the tail return, giving exponent t at step t. It reads each reward at the actual sampled current state and action. The `trajectoryLaw` used at lines 31–33 is separately defined via products of the actual action and transition probabilities; the final unused next-state draw has total probability one. No Bellman identity is assumed to define the finite expectation.
- `stationaryStage` (69–77) copies exactly the model's transition, reward, bounds and the same policy. Replicating this stage N times (89–91) gives stationary finite rollouts with N rewards. It does not select new kernels or policies at later times. `discountedTrajectoryValue_cons` (47–60) proves the first-step decomposition from the trajectory expectation; `stationary_discountedBackup` (80–86) identifies this with the source state Bellman operator, including its policy-induced transition. `finiteDiscountedValue_zero`, `_succ`, and `_eq_iterate` (94–115) give the correct empty and successive finite expectations.
- The norm contraction uses the model's nonnegative γ and strict γ<1; existence and uniqueness are proved on the finite state-function space before the solution is chosen (117–154). `finiteDiscountedValue_error` (157–164) is the geometric estimate γᴺ‖0−Vfixed‖ in the function sup norm, and `tendsto_finiteDiscountedValue` (169–178) proves the finite expectation functions converge to that unique fixed point. The estimate is relative to this fixed point; it is not presented as an already-proved expectation on an infinite path.
- **Infinite trajectory bridge remains open.** No infinite trajectory probability law, measurable infinite return, or proof that its expectation equals the limit of these finite expectations appears here. Consequently this file does not yet establish the source's literal `eq:defn-Vpi-inf` or prove that that literal infinite-path expectation satisfies `eq:bellman_consistency-V`. It establishes the finite path approximations and their fixed-point limit. The Q-function, its conditioned infinite path, and Q-Bellman/source V–Q connection also remain separate coverage obligations.
