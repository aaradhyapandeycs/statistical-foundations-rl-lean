## Independent reconstruction

For a fixed `(s,a)`, the source takes N iid draws from its finite transition row, forms the empirical row, and for a finite set of nonnegative state vectors independent of that row asserts, with probability **greater than** `1−δ`, the simultaneous deviation bound `|(\hat P−P)V| ≤ ‖V‖∞ sqrt(log(2|𝒱|/δ)/(2N))` for `0<δ<1`. The N-term empirical mean requires N>0. For a fixed deterministic V with `0≤V≤B` and B>0, the standard two-sided Hoeffding consequence is `P(|(\hat P−P)V|≥ε) ≤ 2 exp(−2Nε²/B²)` for ε≥0. Setting B=‖V‖∞ is justified for a nonnegative finite vector when its norm is positive. If the norm is zero, V and the deviation vanish pointwise, and the source's zero right-hand side needs separate handling.

## Findings

1. The imported `sampleMeasure` is the actual normalized finite product law of independent identically distributed transition-row draws. `centeredSample` (lines 16–18) subtracts the exact row mean from each sample; `centeredSample_independent` (21–24) derives independence via measurable coordinatewise maps. It does not assume an independent empirical row or replace the iid model by equal marginals.
2. `centeredSample_subgaussian` (27–36) applies Mathlib's bounded-variable Hoeffding lemma to samples in `[0,B]`, with mean identified from the actual coordinate law. Its variance-proxy parameter is `(B/2)^2`; `centeredSum_subgaussian` (39–46) uses actual joint independence to obtain `N(B/2)^2`. The resulting two-sided tail (50–70) covers the two signs by a union bound. The closed event uses `t≤|sum|`; the sum-level bound is valid even in degenerate cases, though its numerical value can be trivial.
3. `centeredSum_eq_empirical` (73–81) proves exact scaling by N for `N>0`. `empiricalRow_hoeffding` (84–111) requires `N>0`, `B>0`, `0≤ε` and the actual range bound and yields the correct exponent `−2Nε²/B²`. The exponent follows from setting the sum threshold to Nε and using proxy `NB²/4`; the proof explicitly justifies cancellation of N and B. At ε=0 the stated upper bound is 2 and hence valid but uninformative.
4. `empiricalRow_hoeffding_norm` (114–122) derives the range `[0,‖V‖]` from nonnegativity and the finite Pi supremum norm, for `‖V‖>0`. `empiricalRow_norm_zero_deviation` (125–130) separately proves zero deviation for all sample paths when `‖V‖=0`; no division by zero is needed for that branch. The additional interval-zero theorem is consistent. The positive norm premise is an internal branch, not a full source lemma with the zero case already combined into its probability statement.
5. **Source lemma remains open.** This file proves a deterministic fixed-vector exponential tail. It does not instantiate the source's logarithmic threshold or `0<δ<1`, take a union bound over a finite family, construct/model a random vector set independent of the empirical row, or prove the source's simultaneous event. It also does not establish the source's strict `P(success)>1−δ`; a non-strict tail bound alone must be handled carefully when deriving the strict probability. The file therefore supports, but does not prove, `lem:Hoeffding-prelim`.
