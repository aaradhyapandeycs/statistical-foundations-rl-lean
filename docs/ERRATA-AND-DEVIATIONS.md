# Errata and deviations

This record compares the formalized statements and proof steps with Chen, Chi, Fan, Li, Wei, and Yan, *Statistical Foundations of Reinforcement Learning: A Non-Asymptotic Perspective*, arXiv:2610.41903v1. Source locations refer to that pinned version, using its LaTeX labels and chapter files. The accompanying manuscript is the [pinned arXiv v1 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.41903v1). Counts are numbered record entries, not the number of repeated occurrences; related occurrences are grouped. Editorial typography is not counted. A proof gap is called an erratum here only when the printed intermediate assertion is false; a missing argument for a true conclusion is described as a proof-route deviation. The source ledger retains additional unresolved candidates that are not counted as confirmed errata.

## arXiv:2610.41903v1

### Errata

1. **Chapter 4, Lemma 4.3, `lemma:TD-step-size`, model-free chapter lines 317–327.** The stated conditions do not include the sample-size and horizon restrictions used for the early-weight estimate. For the source schedule with `γ=999999/1000000`, `c₁=c₂=1/4`, `T=t=100`, and every rate set to the printed lower endpoint `1/(1+c₁(1−γ)T/(log T)^2)`, the initial weight exceeds `1/100²`. The formalized witness is `c5_initial_weight_fails_standalone_bound` in [TDStandaloneStepSizeCounterexample.lean](../Formalization/ModelFree/TDStandaloneStepSizeCounterexample.lean). The added premises appear in [TDStepSizeLate.lean](../Formalization/ModelFree/TDStepSizeLate.lean) and are derived from the enclosing budget in [TDStepSizeBudget.lean](../Formalization/ModelFree/TDStepSizeBudget.lean). **Effect:** the standalone auxiliary lemma is not certified without those premises; the separately stated main TD result is not refuted by this example.

2. **Chapter 5, Lemmas 5.7 and 5.8, `lem:bound-W1-W2-ucbvi-proof` and `lem:bound-T234`, online-RL lines 925–957 and 988–1005.** The auxiliary statements allow the logarithmic endpoint `K=1`. For Lemma 5.7, take `S=A=H=2`, `K=1`, and `δ=1/2`; put all initial mass on state 0, clone the two actions, use zero reward and transition to state 0 at stage 1, and at stage 2 set the rewards to 0 at state 0 and 1 at state 1. Uniform empirical rows then give `W₁=1/2`, while the printed `iota=0`; the event is empty although the claimed confidence is greater than `197/200`. For Lemma 5.8, at `K=H=1`, the strict confidence claim requires probability greater than one. These are source-level endpoint counterexamples; no Lean theorem for them is shipped in this release. The formalized restricted results are [OnlineW123Final.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/Joint/OnlineW123Final.lean) and [OnlineT234Joint.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/Joint/OnlineT234Joint.lean), with their indexed statements in [Ch5.lean](../Formalization/Statements/Ch5.lean). **Effect:** these auxiliary claims require an endpoint restriction or an explicit endpoint repair; the separate main regret theorem handles its one-episode case directly.

3. **Chapter 5, Lemma 5.22, `lemma:sqv`, online-RL lines 3612–3615.** The second variance bound uses coefficient 1 for a general signed random variable. For `X=1` with probability `1/8` and `X=−1` with probability `7/8`, `|X|=1`, `Var(X)=7/16`, and `Var((X−E[X])²)=63/64`, so the printed inequality fails. The Lean counterexample is `centeredSquare_measure_coefficient_one_false` in [CenteredSquareVarianceMeasure.lean](../Formalization/Counterexamples/CenteredSquareVarianceMeasure.lean); coefficient 4 is proved in [SquaredVarianceBounds.lean](../Formalization/Probability/SquaredVarianceBounds.lean). **Effect:** the second clause is replaced by the valid general coefficient-4 bound; the first clause is unchanged.

4. **Chapter 6, Lemma 6.2, `lem:monotone-contraction`, offline-RL lines 872–890.** The displayed standalone domain permits discounts below `1/2`, but the claimed conjunction fails for `γ=0.1`, `c_b=1` on the three-state, one-action example detailed in [SOURCE-ISSUES.md](SOURCE-ISSUES.md#C10). At the visited state `A` the constructed fixed point has `Q̄(A)≈0.9846901842`, while the second iterate has `Q̂₂(A)≈0.9862186757`. The printed uniqueness clause would identify any claimed unique fixed point with the constructed one, contradicting the claimed iterate order. The concrete instance refutes the printed conjunction. No Lean counterexample theorem for this instance is shipped in this release. The appendix invokes `γ≥1/2` at lines 3341–3343, and the corrected statement retains that condition in [MonotoneContractionCorrected.lean](../Formalization/OfflineRL/MonotoneContractionCorrected.lean). **Effect:** only the restricted lemma is formalized. Parent Theorem 6.3 already assumes `γ≥1/2`; this example does not refute that theorem or its rate.

5. **Chapter 5, fixed-profile identification `eq:Vk-Itrue`, online-RL lines 4467–4472.** The printed auxiliary fixed-profile program uses terminal value `H`, while the learner uses terminal value zero. For `S=A=1`, `H=K=2`, zero rewards, and the deterministic sole-state transition, choose `q=min{1/(2c₂),1/(80000 log 6)}`, `δ′=exp(−q)`, and `δ=(4/25)δ′`. The printed budgets hold, but after the first episode both stage rows have count one and the trigger fires. The learner’s stage-2 value is `2c₂q∈(0,1]`, whereas the auxiliary terminal-`H` family is `{0,2}` and omits that value. The source-level construction and scope are detailed in [SOURCE-ISSUES.md](SOURCE-ISSUES.md#C17). No Lean execution counterexample for the printed auxiliary program is shipped in this release. The corrected construction uses a terminal-zero family in [OnlineMVPFixedProfileZeroTerminal.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/MVP/Decoupling/OnlineMVPFixedProfileZeroTerminal.lean), and the actual profile/value bridge is consumed by [OnlineMVPActualEpisodeFixedProfile.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/MVP/Decoupling/OnlineMVPActualEpisodeFixedProfile.lean). **Effect:** the printed auxiliary identification step is not used; this finding does not refute the final Lemma 5.14 or Theorem 5.10 conclusion.

6. **Chapter 5, Theorem 5.16 proof, online-RL lines 2241–2248.** Successive previsit counts are 0,...,N−1, but the displayed equality sums the residuals over 1,...,N. For one visit, the former sum is beta_0=0, whereas an admissible positive beta_1 makes the latter positive. The equality is therefore not valid in general. The formalized proof uses the valid inequality, with beta_0=0 and beta_N≥0, in [VisitShift.lean](../Formalization/Online/QLearning/VisitShift.lean) and [SourceBetaBudget.lean](../Formalization/Online/QLearning/SourceBetaBudget.lean). **Effect:** this corrects an intermediate equality and preserves the final regret rate; it is not a counterexample to Theorem 5.16. See [C20](SOURCE-ISSUES.md#C20) and the [independent source-review record](critic-online-qlearning-source.md).

### Deviations

1. **Chapter 3, Theorem 3.2 and Lemma 3.5, `Thm:sample-compl-main` and `lem:policy-learning-evaluation`, model-based chapter lines 900–923 and 1306–1326.** The formal statements require positive sample count `N>0`. This explicit restriction is recorded in [Ch3.lean](../Formalization/Statements/Ch3.lean). **Effect:** the indexed statements are restricted to the positive-sample domain.

2. **Chapter 3, Theorem 3.9, `Thm:sample-compl-finite`, model-based chapter lines 2551–2570.** The formal statement uses the concrete causal backward-greedy tie rule and positive `N`, rather than every possible data-dependent empirical-optimal tie selection. See [FiniteHorizonMainSample.lean](../Formalization/Generative/FiniteHorizonMainSample.lean) and [Ch3.lean](../Formalization/Statements/Ch3.lean). **Effect:** the result applies to that planner and sample domain.

3. **Chapter 5, UCBVI algorithm and regret bounds, `alg:main-ucbvi` and `thm-ucbvi-regret`, online-RL lines 285–345 and 472–482.** The active algorithm does not define its zero-visit update. The corrected online formalization uses `Q=H` at zero count; its analysis separately charges `H` there, while the empirical transition row keeps its uniform zero-count initialization. This is distinct from the offline LCB commented fallback (mean zero, radius one) disclosed under Theorem 6.1. The online probability results also fix one deterministic maximizing tie. **Effect:** [OnlineUCBVIRegretMain.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/Main/OnlineUCBVIRegretMain.lean) proves the corrected finite completion, not the undefined active branch. The distinction is recorded at [C8](SOURCE-ISSUES.md#C8). The corrected optimism statement permits `0<δ≤1`; the main Theorem 5.2 statement retains `0<δ<1`.

4. **Chapter 5, Theorem 5.2, `thm-ucbvi-regret`, online-RL lines 472–482.** The formalized result supplies explicit universal coefficients `c₁=4`, `c₂=16` and an explicit regret constant 20. **Effect:** this is a concrete witness for the source's universal-constant claim, with the corrected zero-count convention described above. See [OnlineUCBVIRegretMain.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/Main/OnlineUCBVIRegretMain.lean).

5. **Chapter 5, auxiliary joint bounds, `lem:bound-W1-W2-ucbvi-proof`, `lem:bound-T234`, and `lem:bound-T56`, online-RL lines 925–957, 988–1005, and 1055–1092.** Their formalized variants use `K≥2`, which excludes the one-episode endpoints described in Erratum 2. See [OnlineW123Final.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/Joint/OnlineW123Final.lean), [OnlineT234Joint.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/Joint/OnlineT234Joint.lean), and [OnlineT56SourceConfidence.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/Joint/OnlineT56SourceConfidence.lean). **Effect:** the auxiliary statements are restricted; the main regret theorem separately includes `K=1`.

6. **Chapter 5, Algorithm 5.3 and Lemma 5.12, `lem:sum-inv-Nhk-mvp`, online-RL lines 1213–1217, 1280–1288, and 1570–1577.** The formalization follows the doubling prose and uses power-of-two trigger times. The pseudocode's displayed trigger set lists consecutive integers through `2^{⌊log₂K⌋}`, which has a different literal reading; under that reading the printed count bound fails, for example at `S=A=H=1`, `K=32`. The ambiguity and calculation are recorded at [C13](SOURCE-ISSUES.md#C13). See [OnlineMVPArbitraryHistory.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/MVP/OnlineMVPArbitraryHistory.lean). **Effect:** the formalized count theorem is for the power-of-two interpretation, not the literal consecutive-trigger program.

7. **Chapter 5, Lemma 5.11, `lemma:key2`, online-RL lines 1506–1525 and 1540–1563.** The proof counts profiles using a safe ceiling comparison family so it includes every realizable pre-episode profile. It proves the printed final cardinality bound under the printed sample condition, but does not claim the printed intermediate integer inclusion. See [MVPActualProfiles.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/MVP/MVPActualProfiles.lean) and [C15](SOURCE-ISSUES.md#C15). **Effect:** the final bound is unchanged; the intermediate comparison argument is replaced.

8. **Chapter 5, Lemma 5.13, `lemma:uniform`, online-RL lines 1696–1712.** The formalized uniform result uses `δ′=δ/(100S²A²H²K²)` instead of the printed allocation. Its corrected statement retains the displayed concentration coefficients and proves the required family, logarithmic, and variance bounds. See [MVPCorrectedSourceUniformFull.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/MVP/Probability/MVPCorrectedSourceUniformFull.lean) and [C16](SOURCE-ISSUES.md#C16). **Effect:** this is the reciprocal-allocation variant; it does not certify the printed generic allocation or by itself prove actual learner-family membership.

9. **Chapter 5, Lemma 5.14 and Theorem 5.10, `lemma:decouple` and `thm-mvp-regret`, online-RL lines 1722–1744 and 1339–1352.** Their proof uses the corrected terminal-zero fixed-profile family and the actual learner/history bridge. See [OnlineMVPActualEpisodeFixedProfile.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/MVP/Decoupling/OnlineMVPActualEpisodeFixedProfile.lean), [OnlineMVPActualDecouplingSourceConfidence.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/MVP/Decoupling/OnlineMVPActualDecouplingSourceConfidence.lean), and [MVPMainSourceRegret.lean](../Formalization/Online/Regret/MVP/Main/MVPMainSourceRegret.lean). **Effect:** the corrected results are proved without relying on the false fixed-profile identity described in Erratum 5.

10. **Chapter 6, Theorem 6.1, `thm:MAB_LCB_upper`, offline-RL lines 400–443 and 456–471.** At zero count, the formalization selects the commented fallback (empirical mean zero and radius one); it does not treat Lean's totalized division-by-zero value as that branch. See [LCBFallbackApplication.lean](../Formalization/OfflineRL/LCBFallbackApplication.lean) and [C18](SOURCE-ISSUES.md#C18). **Effect:** the theorem is for the explicitly commented completion, not the undefined active pseudocode.

11. **Chapter 6, Lemma 6.2, `lem:monotone-contraction`, offline-RL lines 872–890.** The proved theorem adds `γ≥1/2`, the condition used by the appendix proof. See [MonotoneContractionCorrected.lean](../Formalization/OfflineRL/MonotoneContractionCorrected.lean). **Effect:** the formalized lemma is restricted; it does not alter the parent Theorem 6.3 domain.

12. **Chapter 6, Lemma 6.5, `lem:sample-split-infinite`, offline-RL lines 1104–1115.** The formalized result adds `N≥2` for its union-bound endpoint, using the actual iid triple law and a derived row projection. The parent theorem's sample regime implies this condition. See [SampleSplitActual.lean](../Formalization/OfflineRL/SampleSplitActual.lean) and [C19](SOURCE-ISSUES.md#C19). **Effect:** this is a restricted auxiliary result, not a counterexample to Theorem 6.3.

13. **Chapter 4 universal-constant witnesses, Lemma 4.5 and Theorems 4.1 and 4.6, model-free lines 204–224, 727–743 and 891–912.** The source states existence of universal constants; the formalized proofs choose concrete witnesses. For Lemma 4.5, `c₁=c₂=1/4` and `c₃=4096`; for Theorem 4.1, `c₁=c₂=1/4` and `c₃=max(4096, exp(4)/(log 2)^4)`; for Theorem 4.6, the statement-index witnesses are `c₁=c₂=1` (since 2026-11-09), with the budget constant `c₃` from `qFinal_exists_universal_budget_constant 1`, in [QLearningSourceTheoremClosure.lean](../Formalization/ModelFree/QLearningSourceTheoremClosure.lean); the same witnesses serve the under-estimation claim (`Thm4_6_underestimation`). The earlier proof at `c₁=c₂=1/4` is retained as `qLearning_exists_universal_constants_source_accuracy_quarter`. Schedules with `c₂<1` are not covered by the under-estimation entry; that is a proof-infrastructure limit, not a source error ([review](critic-underestimation.md)). See also [TDSourceVarianceProxyUniversal.lean](../Formalization/ModelFree/TDSourceVarianceProxyUniversal.lean), [TDSourceFullAccuracyUniversal.lean](../Formalization/ModelFree/TDSourceFullAccuracyUniversal.lean), and [QLearningConditionalUniversalConstants.lean](../Formalization/ModelFree/QLearningConditionalUniversalConstants.lean). **Effect:** these are explicit proof witnesses for existential source constants, not changed source assumptions or claimed optimal constants.

14. **Chapter 5, Algorithm `alg:main-q-ucb`, Theorem 5.16 and Lemma 5.18, online-RL lines 1930–1979, 1911–1922 and 2121–2150.** The formalized implementation chooses one fixed deterministic maximizing tie and completes the omitted nonterminal initialization with V=min(H,max Q)=H. The theorem does not quantify over every adaptive or randomized tie selector. It retains the original bonus with cb=2 and supplies residual constant8 and an explicit universal soft-O bound. The complete indexed variants are [Ch5.lean](../Formalization/Statements/Ch5.lean), with applying wrappers in [Ch5Proofs.lean](../Formalization/Statements/Ch5Proofs.lean). **Effect:** the scope is the disclosed completed implementation. The proof retains signed geometric stage weights through concentration and allocates a half confidence budget to each needed event. These are proof-route repairs, not a refutation of the final rate. See [C20](SOURCE-ISSUES.md#C20).

### Proof route and scope

Several source proofs receive supplementary arguments without changing their conclusions. These include a deterministic low-sample case for the omitted intermediate range in the policy-evaluation proof, and a stricter testing estimate that supplies the strict inequality used in the two-state testing reduction. These proof supplements are not counted as errata because the final claims remain valid under the reviewed source conditions. The implementation also proves algorithm-specific bounds through explicit finite probability models and actual trajectory or sample laws rather than treating concentration estimates as assumptions.

This project formalizes selected results and corrected variants, not the complete monograph. In particular, the unrestricted original forms of the statements identified above are not certified merely because a corrected or restricted version is proved. The source-obstruction ledger retains further unresolved candidates; they are not confirmed entries in this record.
