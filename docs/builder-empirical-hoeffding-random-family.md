`randomFiniteFamily_empiricalRow_success_gt_vectorSigma` proves the exact source-radius simultaneous event has probability **strictly greater than `1−δ`** on an arbitrary actual joint probability space. The sample `draw` has the genuine iid `sampleMeasure` law, N is positive, `0<δ<1`, and the finite random family is nonnegative almost surely. Family size may vary with the outcome, including empty families and zero vectors. Its independence premise is independence of the random family from the **empirical row**, not from the ordered sample. The event uses the original square-root radius and the random actual family cardinality.

The empirical row carries `rowVectorSpace`, the measurable structure inherited from its real coordinate vector. `rowVectorSpace_singletons` proves its singleton sets measurable. The proof gives the row a discrete σ-algebra internally for finite-support conditioning, and `indepFun_finiteRange_to_discrete` proves that this does not strengthen independence compared with any measurable-singleton row σ-algebra: the actual empirical row has finite range. Thus the final exported independence hypothesis is explicitly under the ordinary coordinate σ-algebra, not under an unexplained discrete-row strengthening.

`familyTestSpace d N δ` is the σ-algebra generated by the family nonnegativity event and every row-specific family-failure event needed by this bound. The direct theorem requires the random finite-set-valued family to be measurable for, and independent of the row with respect to, this generated family σ-algebra. Its `familyTestSpace_nonneg` and `familyTestSpace_bad` declarations establish the needed section measurability automatically; the final theorem does not assume the probability bound or the success event as a hypothesis. A more general `randomFamily_empiricalRow_success_gt` accepts an arbitrary measurable family-code space and explicit measurability of the finitely many needed failure sections, plus measurability of the nonnegativity event. **No comparison is yet proved between `familyTestSpace` and a separately chosen conventional Borel σ-algebra on finite sets.** The source does not spell out a random-finite-set σ-algebra; a full source-facing audit must address that representation boundary rather than assert that all conventional random-set models have already been bridged.

The proof first pushes the actual iid law to its finite-range empirical-row law and transfers the deterministic-family strict failure bound, separately treating empty families. It forms a measurable supported failure event for the joint family/row law, shows each conditional row-law failure probability is strictly below δ for almost every family, and uses strict monotonicity of the nonnegative integral under the actual family probability law. Independence identifies the actual joint law with the product law. This is a genuine conditional/product argument; it neither conditions on a null event nor assumes family independence of individual raw samples.
