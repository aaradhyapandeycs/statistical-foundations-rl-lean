# Independent review: conditional quadratic MGF ingredient

**Verdict: pass for the stated restricted conditional MGF bound.** This is a preliminary martingale ingredient, not the source Freedman inequality or its sharp constants.

`prelim.tex:1056–1073`, `lem:Freedman-prelim`, requires conditionally centered bounded increments and controls their sum using conditional quadratic variation. The staged theorem concerns a single real random variable X on a probability space, an explicitly supplied sub-σ-algebra `m≤m₀`, an ambient-`m₀` measurable X, `|X|≤B` almost everywhere, `|t|B≤1`, and actual conditional mean `μ[X|m]=0` almost everywhere. Its conclusion is

    E[exp(tX) | m] ≤ exp(t² E[X² | m])     almost everywhere.

The ambient measurability annotation matters: X is not required to be m-measurable, which combined with zero conditional mean would trivialize the intended martingale use. The bound holds for both signs of t, including t=0 and zero conditional variance. There is no separate B≥0 hypothesis; on a probability space the a.e. absolute-value bound makes a negative B impossible, so this does not admit a counterexample. The theorem does not assume an independent sample, the claimed MGF inequality, or an integrability conclusion.

The proof first derives `|tX|≤1` a.e. and uses pinned `Real.norm_exp_sub_one_sub_id_le` to establish the pointwise envelope `exp(tX)≤1+tX+t²X²`. Boundedness, ambient measurability and finite probability measure supply integrability of X, X², the exponential and the envelope; these are proved internally. Conditional-expectation monotonicity and linearity, followed by the given actual conditional mean zero, yield `E[exp(tX)|m]≤1+t²E[X²|m]`; `1+z≤exp z` completes the a.e. bound. The coefficient is **t²**, not the sharper Bernstein coefficient `t²/[2(1−|t|B/3)]`, and the domain `|t|B≤1` is retained explicitly.

The source conditions on the history generated by previous increments. A later application using a larger filtration must establish conditional centering for that filtration; conditioning on the smaller natural history does not automatically imply it for an arbitrary larger one. This file proves no adapted process, exponential supermartingale, variance-restricted tail, optional stopping, peeling union or source probability inequality. In particular the deterministic peeling helper cannot by itself upgrade this MGF coefficient to the source's exact `√8` and `4/3` Freedman constants.
