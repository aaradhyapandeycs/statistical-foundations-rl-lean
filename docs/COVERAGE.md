**Reading conventions (project decision 2026-11-10).** Every source issue, counterexample, refutation,
correction and restriction recorded here is this project's proposal (a candidate found by AI-agent review), not
confirmed by the authors; rows say whether a counterexample or refutation is machine-checked in Lean or a paper
argument. "The Lean proof is this project's own" marks results the book cites or states without proof, and our
corrections and counterexamples; otherwise the Lean proof formalizes the book's argument, with repaired steps linked
to their source issue. Where an entry only asserts that a constant exists, the value quoted is the one the proof
gives ("the proof gives C = …"), not part of the entry. Where the book says "any estimator" and an entry covers only
estimators that are deterministic functions of the data, the entry is a restricted variant.
