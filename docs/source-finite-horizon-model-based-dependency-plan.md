# Finite-horizon model-based theorem: source-first dependency plan

Target: `Thm:sample-compl-finite`, pinned `source/upstream/tex/chapters/generative_model_model_based.tex:2551–2575` (chapter SHA `b7e7abfb2c2cb2c0dd82470db5ecac3a67d7163682246d499935d9130214ac4d`). This is a plan, not an approved frozen declaration or completion claim.

## Exact intended scope

One positive universal constant precedes finite nonempty state/action carriers, horizon H, known stage rewards in [0,1], stage-dependent transition kernels, sample count N, confidence δ and accuracy ε. The printed domain is δ>0 and0<ε≤H. The algorithm collects N independent next states for every (stage,state,action), forms actual count/N empirical transitions (`eq:defn-empirical-P-finite`) and performs exact empirical-model planning. Total count is HSA N. The final **single** confidence event must contain both actual trajectory guarantees, simultaneously at every stage: `V_h^πhat≥V_h*−ε` and `Q_h^πhat≥Q_h*−ε`. No discounted γ factor belongs in the Q tolerance. The per-tuple budget is exactly `N≥c₀H³log(HSA/δ)/ε²`.

Preserve all exact deterministic empirical-optimal tie choices; do not silently freeze one selector when the intended algorithm allows arbitrary optima. Define empirical optimality at every state and stage, not merely one starting state or initial distribution. ε≤H implies H>0; positive N remains the empirical frequency/log domain. δ≥1 has a trivial at-least1−δ conclusion and should be handled separately rather than narrowing the source wrapper to δ<1. H=1 with known rewards is exact and can avoid artificial logarithmic comparison restrictions. Terminal time H+1 has value0 and is not another sampled decision stage. The source samples all H stage rows, including the last stage's statistically unnecessary transition; omitting them changes the printed experiment unless an explicit marginal-equivalence argument is given.

## New bounded foundation under development

`.local/FiniteHorizonGenerativeSampling.lean` defines a full product law indexed by `(Fin H×State×Action)×Fin N`, true coordinate marginals and independence, full row iid pushforwards, empirical environment stages preserving known reward, exact empirical transition expectations, and independence of the strictly-future sample block from each complete current row. A generic future-code statistic is independent by measurable composition. This is deliberately weaker than asserting that a fitted continuation value factors through the future block; that factorization must be proved from backward recursion. It is nonduplicative: no existing finite-stage experiment module was found, and the new law uses genuinely stage-varying transition kernels without imposing a discounted-MDP carrier.

## Remaining bounded dependency tasks

1. **Stage-list alignment / suffix factorization.** Connect empirical stage arrays to the existing environment list with length H and consistent Fin/zero-based suffix indices. Prove canonical optimal continuation value at stage h+1 is a deterministic function solely of data at later stages. This is a recursion theorem, not an independence premise. Then compose with future-block independence to obtain actual independence from the entire current stage/state/action row.
2. **One common row concentration event.** Apply fixed/random-family Bernstein with actual row law and that independently constructed future statistic, union over stages/states/actions. Quantify the canonical empirical optimal continuation values, whose equality is independent of tie choices. Prove all exact empirical-optimal deterministic policies share those empirical suffix values. Arbitrary tie policies themselves may depend on earlier data; do not assert their independence just because canonical backward values are independent.
3. **Exact simulation identities and performance transfer.** Prove finite-horizon true/empirical policy-evaluation difference recursion/telescoping for the same nonstationary policy and known rewards, then empirical-optimality performance decomposition at every suffix. Maintain actual forced-first-action Q semantics for all actions. Propagating a fixed uniform next-stage V-loss through an undiscounted transition preserves that tolerance, but stagewise statistical terms must still be controlled.
4. **Sharp cumulative variance.** Establish the finite-horizon total conditional-variance/energy bound along actual transition occupancy and its empirical-value interaction. Avoid a crude H-fold sup variance substitution that produces an extra H factor. Any self-bound, martingale or Cauchy–Schwarz/Jensen step must be proved with precise remaining-horizon indices and dependencies, not introduced as a final theorem hypothesis.
5. **Probability/constants assembly.** Build one event for simultaneous V/Q and all exact optimizers, include the positive-domain/one-stage/δ≥1 branches, derive the exact printed budget with a universal constant and prove all logarithm/cardinality comparisons. Do not transplant discounted β or its N-dependent log as though it were the finite source's log(HSA/δ).
