It has no `sorry`, `admit`, custom axiom, or assumed equality of the two laws.

`infiniteTrajectoryLaw_map_replicatePrefix` states that for every `n : ℕ`, including `n = 0`, the pushforward of the actual Ionescu–Tulcea infinite controlled trajectory law under `finitePrefix (List.replicate n (M.stationaryStage π))` equals the PMF-backed measure of the independently constructed `DecisionStage.trajectoryLaw` for those same n repeated stationary stages and initial state. The prefix map is an explicit total function into the actual dependent `FinitePath` carrier. `finitePrefix_cons_head` proves its first pair is trajectory index 0; `finitePrefix_cons_tail` proves its tail is the encoded prefix of the shifted infinite trajectory. This checks its index order rather than relying only on measure equality.

The proof compares two independently constructed laws. It first proves `historyMass_first_step`, turning the earlier Iic-indexed cylinder product into a head/tail recursion. `historyToFinitePath` and `finitePathToHistory` are inverse maps, packaged as `historyFinitePathEquiv`. `historyMass_eq_finitePathWeight` proves equality of the cylinder mass and the actual finite `DecisionStage.pathWeight` for any all-stationary list of stages; the stage-list hypothesis is discharged for the explicit `List.replicate n` theorem. Singleton measure extensionality and the existing cylinder theorem yield `infiniteTrajectoryLaw_map_historyFinitePath`, then `infiniteTrajectoryLaw_map_finitePrefix` proves equality for any stationary-stage list and handles its empty case. No dependent `List.replicate` length cast is needed.
