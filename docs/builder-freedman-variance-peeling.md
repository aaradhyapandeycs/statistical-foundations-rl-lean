## Exact source interface

`lem:Freedman-prelim`, `source/upstream/tex/chapters/prelim.tex:1056–1080`, has source-file SHA `2d27bb720d49020ce1d3ea362b1678a90c6208882accca90fef21c25e42031a5`. It concerns a scalar real martingale-difference sequence Xₖ, |Xₖ|≤B and conditional mean zero given its past; Yₙ is its actual sum and Wₙ is the sum of actual conditional second moments. It assumes a deterministic upper bound Wₙ≤σ². For every positive integer K≥1 and 0<δ<1 it asserts probability at least1−δ of

`|Yₙ|≤sqrt(8 max(Wₙ,σ²/2^K) log(2K/δ))+(4/3)B log(2K/δ)`.

The random variance Wₙ remains inside the radius. Replacing it by σ² everywhere loses the advertised peeling refinement. K is positive, not an arbitrary real exponent, and the logarithm pays for K bins and two tails. The theorem is at one fixed n; no extra uniform-in-time conclusion is printed. A future Lean declaration must state the probability measure/filtration, adaptedness, integrability and conditional identities explicitly. B and σ² are deterministic scales; nonnegative domains should be explicit. Conditional mean zero with respect to the sigma-algebra generated by past X does not automatically imply it for a larger history filtration; applications should prove their fresh-sample mean-zero identity for the actual chosen filtration. Almost-everywhere versions of boundedness/conditional-variance bounds faithfully suffice for probability conclusions, but do not permit arbitrary versions on non-null sets.

## Existing pinned APIs and missing layer

Pinned Lean is v4.27.0-rc1; Mathlib revision `32d24245c7a12ded17325299fd41d412022cd3fe`. Searches of Probability and Analysis found no existing named Freedman/Bennett martingale bound or this K-bin variance cover. Mathlib `Probability/Moments/SubGaussian.lean` provides `ProbabilityTheory.Martingale.measure_sum_ge_le_of_HasCondSubgaussianMGF` and the corresponding sum-MGF construction. These use conditional subGaussian parameters and yield an Azuma-style deterministic proxy; they do not establish a variance-sensitive bounded-increment Freedman inequality. `Probability/Martingale/Basic.lean` and conditional-expectation APIs provide the adapted process/conditional mean infrastructure. Project `EmpiricalBernstein.lean` proves iid row MGF bounds and factorization; these cannot be applied to a sum of random-iterate martingale increments by asserting independence.

Consequently the full stochastic bounded-increment exponential-supermartingale/tail theorem remains missing. No such theorem is assumed by this helper.

## Three completed deterministic exports

All live in `StatisticalRL`.

- `dyadic_variance_div_step`: exact `(v/2)/2^j=v/2^(j+1)`.
- `dyadic_variance_bin_cover`: for a positive integer K and 0≤w≤v, there exists j<K with `w≤v/2^j≤2max(w,v/2^K)`.
- `dyadic_variance_radius_cover`: with an additional deterministic L≥0, the same selected bin obeys `sqrt(4(v/2^j)L)≤sqrt(8max(w,v/2^K)L)`.

The first two domains include v=w=0, K=1, w=v, the bottom cutoff, and every dyadic boundary. Bins may overlap at boundaries; coverage is what a union bound needs, not disjointness. No division by w or v occurs. This is useful for exact source peeling: cover all possible conditional variances by K upper-bin scales, use a separately proved variance-restricted tail bound on each bin, then transfer its radius to the printed random-variance radius. The helper only proves numerical coverage/comparison; it does not fabricate a fixed-variance tail theorem, supply its constants, or spend confidence. In particular the factor4 in the numerical comparison is not a claim that a suitable probabilistic base inequality has already been formalized.
