# Statistical Foundations of Reinforcement Learning in Lean

**Work in progress. The entire monograph is not yet formalized.**

This project aims to formalize the whole of Chen, Chi, Fan, Li, Wei, and Yan,
*Statistical Foundations of Reinforcement Learning: A Non-Asymptotic Perspective*,
[arXiv:2610.41903v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41903v1).
The scope includes model-based and model-free RL with a generative model,
online RL, offline RL, the extensions, and all mathematical appendices.

Builder agents implement definitions and proofs. Independent critic agents
check the mathematics, source correspondence, hidden assumptions, and actual
proof dependencies. Compiling a smaller infrastructure library does not
establish completion of the monograph.

## Reproduce

Lean `v4.27.0-rc1` and Mathlib commit
`32d24245c7a12ded17325299fd41d412022cd3fe` are pinned.

```sh
python3 scripts/fetch_source.py
lake exe cache get
lake build
lake env lean Audit.lean
```

The source provenance and license are recorded in [source/](source/README.md).
Current work and open obligations are recorded in [docs/PROGRESS.md](docs/PROGRESS.md).
The [source checklist](docs/source-inventory.md) does not itself certify proof
coverage; the [reviewed coverage ledger](docs/COVERAGE.md) maps current results
to source statements and records their exact limits.

Every one of the 90 numbered results in the inventory now has a reviewed Lean record, but that record is
*a proved form, possibly corrected or restricted*, not necessarily the printed statement; the status page
separates printed, corrected, restricted and refuted forms (44 printed, 29 corrected and 17 restricted, as
generated by `scripts/gen_nodes.py`). For Theorem 7.11 the counted form is corrected and restricted, and is
counted as restricted: true noise covariance instead of the printed one, closed half-spaces only, and an
instance-dependent constant. Over all convex sets only a partial bound K·T^(-1/3)·log(2T) is proved; the printed
rate over all convex sets remains open. The monograph as a whole is **incomplete**: prose
claims, algorithms and displays are tracked separately, and no result is author-approved. Remarks and
displays recorded as implied by a proved entry, explanatory, or cited and out of scope (a project decision of
2026-11-09) are listed separately and are never counted as proved.

Only the standard foundational axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and
`Quot.sound` are allowed in the transitive dependency closure. No source theorem
may be claimed by assuming its missing intermediate steps.

The GitHub Actions workflow checks the pinned source, inventory, scanner tests,
Lean build, and project axiom audit on each push. Its presence does not by itself
establish that a particular run has passed. The same commands and
`python3 scripts/check.py` can be run locally.

## Live progress

Run `python3 scripts/progress_dashboard.py` and open
`http://127.0.0.1:8765/` on the same computer. The read-only dashboard refreshes
local Lean line counts and a conservative reviewed-named-result ledger every
10 seconds. Its percentage is not an estimate of all mathematical work in the
monograph. The locally recorded `origin/main` snapshot may lag GitHub until
the next fetch or push.

## Errata and deviations

For the pinned source arXiv:2610.41903v1, the counts of errata and deviations are kept in the table at the top of [docs/ERRATA-AND-DEVIATIONS.md](docs/ERRATA-AND-DEVIATIONS.md). The entries come from the candidates in [docs/SOURCE-ISSUES.md](docs/SOURCE-ISSUES.md), found by independent AI-agent review against the pinned TeX; none is confirmed by the authors. The book reader site also lists them on its generated "Source issues (candidates)" page. Errata, meaning printed statements or proof steps shown false, include the domains of Theorems 3.7, 4.12, 4.13, 7.3, 7.5 and 7.8, the regret-to-PAC conversion constant, the standalone TD step-size bound, the `K=1` endpoints of auxiliary online bounds, the MVP factor-two count step and uniform confidence allocation, the signed-square variance coefficient, a false fixed-profile value-identification step, the online Q-learning previsit-beta equality, the discount domain of Lemma 6.2, the single-logarithm sample condition of Theorem 6.8 and premises of Lemmas 6.10–6.12, the DRVI iteration count of Theorems 7.2 and 7.4, the unrestricted planning oracle of Theorem 7.6, the learning rate of Theorem 7.9, the covariance of Theorem 7.11 and the ill-posed update of Theorem 7.12. Most of these have named Lean refutations; the record says which are paper arguments only. For Theorem 7.11 only a corrected and restricted variant on closed half-spaces is proved (true covariance, instance-dependent constant); over all convex sets a partial bound with an extra log(2T) factor is proved, and the printed rate is open. The deviations disclose the positive-sample scope, finite zero-count conventions, the power-of-two MVP trigger interpretation, corrected profile and confidence constructions, the commented fallback used for Theorem 6.1, explicit Chapter 4 constant witnesses, the fixed-tie/nonterminal-value completion of online Q-learning, and the constant reading and exact-averaging restriction of Theorem 7.10. See [the full record](docs/ERRATA-AND-DEVIATIONS.md).
