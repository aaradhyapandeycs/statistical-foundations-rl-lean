# Statistical Foundations of Reinforcement Learning in Lean

**Work in progress. The entire monograph is not yet formalized.**

This project aims to formalize the whole of Chen, Chi, Fan, Li, Wei, and Yan,
*Statistical Foundations of Reinforcement Learning: A Non-Asymptotic Perspective*,
[arXiv:2610.41903v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41903v1).
The scope includes model-based and model-free RL with a generative model,
online RL, offline RL, the extensions, and all mathematical appendices.

Builder agents implement definitions and proofs. Independent critic agents
check the mathematics, source correspondence, hidden assumptions, and actual
proof dependencies. Compiling a smaller infrastructure library does not
establish completion of the monograph.

## Reproduce

Lean `v4.27.0-rc1` and Mathlib commit
`32d24245c7a12ded17325299fd41d412022cd3fe` are pinned.

```sh
python3 scripts/fetch_source.py
lake exe cache get
lake build
lake env lean Audit.lean
```

The source provenance and license are recorded in [source/](source/README.md).
Current work and open obligations are recorded in [docs/PROGRESS.md](docs/PROGRESS.md).
The [source checklist](docs/source-inventory.md) does not itself certify proof
coverage; the [reviewed coverage ledger](docs/COVERAGE.md) maps current results
to source statements and records their exact limits.

Current counts are not written here, because they go stale. Every count comes from one inventory: the
`summary` of [docs/graph/nodes.json](docs/graph/nodes.json), generated by `python3 scripts/gen_nodes.py`
(print it with `python3 scripts/gen_nodes.py --summary`) and shown on the reader site's
[Progress page](https://aaradhyapandeycs.github.io/statistical-foundations-rl-lean/status.html). Each source item
gets one of four labels: *proved as stated*, *proved with corrections*, *proved under restrictions* or
*incomplete*, plus a marker when Lean refutes the printed statement. A record in the ledger is a proved form,
possibly corrected or restricted, not necessarily the printed statement. The monograph as a whole is
**incomplete**: prose claims, algorithms and displays are tracked separately, and no result is author-approved.
Remarks and displays recorded as implied by a proved entry, explanatory, or cited and out of scope (a project
decision of 2026-11-09) are never counted as proved.

Only the standard foundational axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and
`Quot.sound` are allowed in the transitive dependency closure. No source theorem
may be claimed by assuming its missing intermediate steps.

The GitHub Actions workflow checks the pinned source, inventory, scanner tests,
Lean build, and project axiom audit on each push. Its presence does not by itself
establish that a particular run has passed. The same commands and
`python3 scripts/check.py` can be run locally.

## Live progress

Run `python3 scripts/progress_dashboard.py` and open
`http://127.0.0.1:8765/` on the same computer. The read-only dashboard refreshes
local Lean line counts and the numbered-result label counts from
`scripts/gen_nodes.py` every 10 seconds. These counts are not an estimate of all
mathematical work in the monograph. The locally recorded `origin/main` snapshot may lag GitHub until
the next fetch or push.

## Errata and deviations

For the pinned source arXiv:2610.41903v1, the counts are kept in the table at the top of [docs/ERRATA-AND-DEVIATIONS.md](docs/ERRATA-AND-DEVIATIONS.md). The entries come from candidates in [docs/SOURCE-ISSUES.md](docs/SOURCE-ISSUES.md), found by independent AI-agent review against the pinned TeX; none is confirmed by the authors. They are classed A to D: four printed statements (Lemma 5.22, Theorem 6.8, Theorems 7.2 and 7.4, and the rectangle confidence-set recipe of Chapter 7) appear false in a non-degenerate case under their natural reading (class A); further items fail only at a boundary case or outside a parameter range assumed elsewhere (B), contain a false proof step whose conclusion survives (C), or are ambiguous, ill-posed or undefined, so that Lean refutes them only under a stated reading or uses a convention for an undefined value (D, questions for the authors). The book reader marks only the first kind as refuted and gives the others their own markers. The deviations disclose scope restrictions, conventions, interpretations and explicit constant witnesses. See [the full record](docs/ERRATA-AND-DEVIATIONS.md).
